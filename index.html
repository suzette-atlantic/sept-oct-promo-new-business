Before you book

What new partners need to know.

Who is this offer for?

New business. This independent promo is for organizations starting a custom training relationship with Atlantic, not for existing customer expansions.

When does it run?

Offer valid September 1 through October 31, 2026. All contracts must be signed by October 31, 2026.

Is the Course Builder Workshop really on every contract?

Yes. Every new-business contract in this promo includes a free 1-hour Course Builder Workshop to map your custom curriculum.

What is a free enrollment topic?

On qualifying contracts over $30,000, you receive a free enrollment topic valued at $2,250. Qualifying uses may include new hire onboarding, new facilities, department growth, or workforce expansion.

What is Hall Pass bonus capacity?

On contracts under $15,000, you unlock bonus seats or Trainer Streaming for 90 days so more people can train while the program is getting off the ground.
